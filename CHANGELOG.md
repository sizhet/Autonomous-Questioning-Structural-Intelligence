# Changelog

All notable changes to **Autonomous Questioning Structural Intelligence (AQSI)** are documented here.

This repository is a **theory-first research framework**. The changelog describes research documents, proposed interfaces, and publication assets; it does **not** imply that an AQSI runtime, experiments, or performance improvements have been implemented or validated.

The format follows the principles of [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/) and uses semantic-style version labels for research releases.

## [1.0.0] — 2026-10-29

### Added — Research foundation

- **AQSI-001** — *From Answering to Autonomous Questioning*: motivation, scope, functional domains, and research boundaries.
- **AQSI-002** — *Biomimetic Question Discovery*: gap-, residual-, association-, and relay-driven candidate generation, including structure-first and LLM-first paths.
- **AQSI-003** — *Multi-Granularity Structural Influence Factors*: typed heterogeneous factors, directional relationships, and influence folding.
- **AQSI-004** — *Historical Question Intelligence and Differential Trees*: historical outcomes, question families, screening, delayed value, and revisit mechanisms.
- **AQSI-005** — *Directional Question Scoring and Portfolio Selection*: goal-open scoring, uncertainty-aware influence aggregation, and resource-constrained portfolios.
- **AQSI-006** — *Question Units and Exploration Contracts*: durable research objects, expression adapters, evidence expectations, and exploration boundaries.
- **AQSI-007** — *Question Structural Relay and Collective Learning*: derivation, transfer, replication, counterevidence, provenance, and collective reuse.
- **AQSI-008** — *Strategic Question Control Planes*: human strategic authority, portfolio policy, tactical selection, and operational authorization.
- **AQSI-009** — *UTN and Cross-Domain Question Transfer*: typed alignment, conditional transfer, historical reuse, and negative-transfer safeguards.

### Research principles established

- **Specifications and contracts before runtime claims.**
- **Question identity is distinct from prompt expression.**
- **Estimated structural influence is distinct from verified research value.**
- **Unknown relationships must not silently be treated as zero influence.**
- **Question selection does not confer action authorization.**
- **Multi-agent interaction alone does not establish collective learning.**
- **Open-ended questioning does not demonstrate recursive intelligence improvement or ASI.**
- **Evidence, counterevidence, explicit leftovers, and provenance remain first-class research records.**

## Versioning policy

- **Patch** (`1.0.x`): editorial corrections, broken links, typographical fixes, and clarifications that do not materially alter contracts.
- **Minor** (`1.x.0`): backward-compatible research additions, new hypotheses, examples, or optional contract fields.
- **Major** (`x.0.0`): substantive revisions to the conceptual architecture, canonical contracts, governance boundaries, or compatibility assumptions.

When revisions change a hypothesis or interpretation, preserve a traceable record of the previous formulation, the new evidence or argument, and any remaining disagreement.

## Contributing changes

A proposed research change should identify: (1) affected document and section, (2) claim or contract being changed, (3) rationale and evidence, (4) counterevidence or limitations, (5) compatibility implications, and (6) relevant tests or validation work. Negative results and unresolved objections are welcome and should remain discoverable.
